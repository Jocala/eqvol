Jocala eqVol

HDMI Volume for the Mac

macOS treats most HDMI displays as fixed-volume audio outputs: the volume keys do nothing, and there is no CEC to bridge the gap. eqVol publishes a virtual audio device, routes it through a small clock-locked engine, and mirrors macOS volume into your display over HDMI — so the keyboard volume keys, a menu-bar slider, and osascript all just work. Free, notarized, and universal (Apple Silicon + Intel). No ads, no tracking, nothing leaves your Mac.

Get eqVol 1.1

macOS 14.2 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel. The download is a signed and notarized disk image: open it and run Install eqVol.pkg. The installer asks for your administrator password once (the virtual audio driver installs system-wide), and macOS may ask to allow audio capture — click Allow.

How it works

eqVol installs a virtual audio driver, makes it the default output, and taps the device's output mix (never a microphone). A varispeed engine compensates for clock drift between your Mac and the display, then writes volume and mute changes straight into the signal path — giving any HDMI monitor the volume control it never had.
