eqVol — HDMI Volume for the Mac

This installer sets up eqVol on your Mac:

  EqVol.app            -> /Applications (menu-bar app)
  virtual audio driver -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL
  background engine    -> /Library/Application Support/eqVol
  autostart            -> your LaunchAgents (starts at login)

Your administrator password is required (the audio driver installs
system-wide). Afterwards, if macOS asks about audio capture, click
Allow (one time) — eqVol taps only its own virtual device, never a
microphone.
